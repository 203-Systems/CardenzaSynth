# Playing

The 4×8 **PADS** always play the active track. Keyboard layout, scale, root note, octave, velocity, and scale lock are shared across all four tracks and configured on <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="KEYS" /> (see [KEYS](./setup.mdx#setup-keys)). Chords and the arpeggiator (ARP) are per-track, with dedicated **CHORD** and **ARP** pages in <Scene name="INSTR" /> for every melodic track. All of these settings save with your project; creating a NEW project resets them to PIANO layout, C MAJOR, chords off, and arpeggiators off.

## Keyboard layouts {#play-keyboard}

Melodic tracks support three keyboard layouts (set via LAYOUT on the KEYS page), and the pad LEDs indicate which keys are playable:

  <KeyMark keys="r2c2 r2c3 r2c5 r2c6 r2c7">Black keys (lower octave)</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="r3c1-r3c8">White keys C–C (C3 at bottom left)</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="r0c1 r0c4 r0c8 r2c1 r2c4 r2c8">Unmapped gaps: off</KeyMark>
  <Caption>Default PIANO layout: two octaves stacked in pairs of rows, with black keys above white keys and unused gaps unlit.</Caption>

| Layout | Note mapping | LEDs |
|---|---|---|
| **PIANO** (default) | Two full octaves: bottom two rows start at C3 (bottom left), top two rows are one octave up. White keys (C D E F G A B C) sit on the lower row of each pair, black keys on the upper row. Gaps with no black key (before C#, between E and F, after A#) are inactive. | Gaps <Led state="off" /> |
| **SCALE** | Only notes in the chosen scale, ascending left to right from the root, with each row one octave higher. Every pad plays an in-scale note. | All <Led state="half" /> |
| **CHROMA** | Chromatic layout, ascending one semitone per key from the root, left to right and bottom to top. | All <Led state="half" /> |

| <Led state="half">half lit</Led> | Playable key |

## Octave {#play-octave}

**OCTAVE** on the KEYS page shifts the keyboard from −3 to +3 octaves. You can also adjust it from any screen with the quick shortcut:

## Velocity {#play-velocity}

The pads are not velocity-sensitive. On melodic tracks, all keys play at the velocity set by **VELO** on the KEYS page (1–127, default 110). Recorded notes inherit this velocity, and you can edit individual step velocities later in the sequencer using the VEL lane (see [Selecting and editing](./seq.mdx#seq-edit)). On drum tracks, pad rows select velocity instead (see [Drums](#play-drums)).

<Keywords k="velocity, dynamics, how hard, loudness of a note, 力度" />

## Scale lock {#play-lock}

In PIANO and CHROMA layouts, **LOCK** controls what happens when you press a note outside the selected scale:

| LOCK | Behavior for out-of-scale keys | LED |
|---|---|---|
| **OFF** | Plays normally | <Led state="half" /> |
| **MUTE** | Silent | <Led state="off" /> |
| **SNAP** | Plays the nearest scale note below it | <Led state="half" /> |

## Chords {#play-chords}

The **CHORD** page in <Scene name="INSTR" /> lets you trigger full chords from a single pad. Chord settings belong to the track, so they persist even if you change presets or swap engines. Drum tracks do not have a CHORD page.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G2" shot="instr-chord" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" />, hold <Key k="CTRL" /> and press G2 to open CHORD (off by default).</Step>
  <Step keys="G1" shot="chord-on" at="cell:1,1 9,43,170,20">Tap CHORD to switch to TRIAD. The screen shows the notes triggered by the root key (`C3 PLAYS C3 E3 G3`).</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r3c1~C" shot="chord-held" at="cells:3,1-4,4">Press a pad to play all three chord tones at once; the triggered notes light up on both the screen and the pads.</Step>
</Strip>

- **CHORD**: OFF, TRIAD, 7TH, MAJ, MIN, SUS4, MAJ7, MIN7, DOM7, POWER, or OCTAVE. TRIAD and 7TH stack thirds diatonically within the current scale (out-of-scale keys fall back to a major triad or dominant seventh); all other modes play fixed chord shapes.
- **VOICING**: ROOT (root position), INV1 (first inversion: lowest note up an octave), INV2 (second inversion: lowest two notes up an octave), or SPREAD (second note up an octave).
- The second line lists the diatonic triad for every degree of the current scale (in all-caps font: minor appears as `D-`, diminished as `BDIM`).
- During live recording, chord tones are recorded as individual notes, and the arpeggiator steps through them one by one. In the SEQ note editor, tapping a pad toggles the entire chord as a group (see [Selecting and editing](./seq.mdx#seq-edit)).

## Arpeggiator {#play-arp}

The **ARP** page in <Scene name="INSTR" /> steps through held notes sequentially. Each melodic track has an independent arpeggiator, and multiple tracks can arpeggiate simultaneously (for example, you can latch an arpeggio with HOLD on T2 while playing T3 live).

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G3" shot="instr-arp" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" />, hold <Key k="CTRL" /> and press G3 to open ARP. The step display stays dimmed while ARP is OFF.</Step>
  <Step keys="G1, hold r3c1~C + r3c3~E + r3c5~G" shot="arp-running" at="cells:3,1-4,4">Tap ARP to select UP, then hold C, E, and G to arpeggiate them from lowest to highest.</Step>
</Strip>

  <Mark at="cell:1,3">RANGE</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:1,4">GATE</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:2,1">HOLD (latch)</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,1-4,4">16-step playback preview</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="r3c1 r3c3 r3c5">Held keys</KeyMark>
  <Caption>ARP set to UP while holding C, E, and G. Each bar shows a step's pitch and gate length; the white bar marks the currently playing step.</Caption>

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| ARP | OFF, UP, DOWN, UP-DN, RANDOM, ORDER (order pressed) |
| RATE | 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16 (default), 1/16T, 1/32 |
| RANGE | 1–4 octaves (upward) |
| GATE | 10–100% of step length (default 50%) |
| HOLD | Latches held notes after release; pressing new keys after releasing all keys starts a new pattern. A single tap toggles HOLD immediately. |

- Up to 16 notes (including generated chord tones) can be arpeggiated. The first note triggers immediately.
- When the transport is stopped, the arpeggiator runs freely at the current BPM. During playback, it locks to the sequencer clock (1/16 steps follow global swing), and starting or stopping playback does not interrupt its timing.
- UP, DOWN, and UP-DN continue from the last note played without repeating turnaround notes.
- When live recording (REC) is enabled during playback, the arpeggiator's output is recorded into the track's pattern using the current GATE length.
- Switching tracks leaves other tracks' arpeggiators running. Turning HOLD off stops latched notes immediately while allowing currently held keys to keep playing. Pressing <Key k="FN+ESC" /> (All Stop) or loading a project stops all arpeggiators.

## Drums {#play-drums}

On drum tracks (<Track n="1" /> by default), each pad **column** triggers one drum and each **row** sets its velocity: 127 on the top row, then 100, 70, and 40 on the bottom row. Idle pads stay half lit; holding a pad lights its column from the bottom up to that row, and incoming hits animate up the column proportionally to velocity. On screen, pad graphics use four dither densities to reflect hit strength. Drum tracks do not use scales, chords, VELO, or the arpeggiator. Adjust individual drum volumes on the INSTR KIT page (see [DRUMS](./instr.mdx#engine-drums)).
