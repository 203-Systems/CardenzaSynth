# 3 TAPE

**TAPE** brings all four tracks together: clip launching, song arrangement, mixing, effect sends, and master bus effects. Step through its five pages with <Key k="CTRL" />:

<Page name="SESSION" /> <Page name="SONG" /> <Page name="MIX" /> <Page name="SENDS" /> <Page name="FX" />

Project saving, loading, and global tempo live in <Scene name="SETUP" /> (see [PROJECT](./setup.mdx#setup-project) and [FILES](./setup.mdx#setup-files)). Everything on TAPE except master output volume is saved inside your project. Row 4 of the FN menu stays consistent across every TAPE page: SAVE (<Key k="FN+S" />), TAP (<Key k="FN+T" />), REC (<Key k="FN+PLAY" />), and SETUP.

<Keywords k="tape, song, arrangement, mixer, whole song, four tracks, 走带" />

<Strip>
  <Step keys="PLAY, G6" shot="tape-session-queued" at="cell:2,2">Tap a cell during playback to queue that pattern for the end of the track's current loop. The cell displays `NXT` and its key <Led state="blink">blinks</Led>.</Step>

- Tap any cell to launch that pattern on its track (and focus that track). Tapping the `+` cell below a track's last pattern creates a new empty pattern and launches it.
- Use <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to move the row cursor up or down and scroll; press <Key k="OK" /> to launch the entire cursor row across all tracks that have a pattern in that slot.
- Press <Key k="FN+OK" /> to capture the currently playing (or queued) combination of patterns as a new section at the end of **SONG**. The new section's bar length matches its longest pattern.
- The FN menu includes **LAUNCH** (trigger cursor row) and **ADD** TO SONG (same as <Key k="FN+OK" />).
- Launching patterns from SESSION switches out of SONG mode into LOOP mode. Active playing cells highlight green with `▶` and a moving playhead.

<Keywords k="session, clip, launch, scene launch, ableton, live, grid launch" />

## SONG: arranging the song {#tape-song}

SONG lays out your arrangement from left to right: each **column** is a song section (part), each **row** is a track, and each cell holds the pattern number for that track along with the section's length in bars (`xN`).

<Shot name="tape-song">
  <Mark at="cells:1,1-4,1">Part 1</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:1,2-4,2">Part 2</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:1,1">Selected cell</Mark>
  <Mark at="47,15,17,10">Length in bars (xN)</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:1,3-4,3">+ column: append a new part</Mark>
  <Mark at="199,111,31,12">LOOP / SONG mode indicator</Mark>
  <Caption>SONG view: each column is a section, each row is a track.</Caption>
</Shot>

- Tap a cell to select it; tap the same cell again to toggle focus between the track's **pattern** and the section's **bar length** (bar count highlights yellow when focused). `--` leaves a track silent for that section.
- Tap the `+` column after the final section to append a copy of the last section (or, on an empty song, the patterns currently active on each track).
- Use <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to change the focused pattern (including `--`) or bar length (1–16). <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> always adjust bar length regardless of focus.
- Press <Key k="OK" /> to play from the selected section (activates SONG mode; jumps to that section if already playing). Press <Key k="FN+OK" /> to toggle between SONG and LOOP modes.
- Press <Key k="FN+C" />, <Key k="FN+V" />, <Key k="FN+X" />, or <Key k="FN+D" /> to copy, paste (inserting after the cursor), delete, or duplicate a section. All four actions are also in the FN menu (labeled `ROW n`) alongside **PLAY** (SONG/LOOP toggle).
- The **PLAY** switch on [PROJECT](./setup.mdx#setup-project) controls the same SONG/LOOP mode. Because it is a performance switch, it is not saved in the project file, and launching a clip in SESSION automatically returns to LOOP mode.

In **SONG** mode, each section plays for `xN` bars (1 bar = 16 steps) before advancing to the next and looping back to the start after the last section. In **LOOP** mode, each track loops its active pattern independently. The currently playing section displays a green playhead, and a queued section is framed in yellow.

:::tip

Jam in SESSION until you find a combination you like, then press <Key k="FN+OK" /> to append it straight to SONG.

:::

- Each GRID column maps to one track: row 1 selects the track, row 2 toggles **MUTE**, row 3 toggles **SOLO**, and row 4 toggles **SEQ** (sequencer playback on/off).
- Use <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to adjust the selected track's volume (0–100, default 100; hold <Key k="FN" /> for ±10). Press <Key k="OK" /> to switch fader focus between the selected track and **MASTER**. Hold a track's row-1 key to pick its level directly across the 32 pads.
- **MASTER** adjusts the hardware output volume (identical to **VOLUME** on [SYSTEM](./setup.mdx#setup-system)), which is stored globally as a device setting. Individual track levels, mute, solo, and SEQ states are saved with the project.
- The FN menu includes **UNMUTE ALL** and **UNSOLO ALL** whenever any track is muted or soloed.

:::tip

Holding <Key k="TAB" /> opens this exact same track/mute/solo/SEQ layout from any screen.

:::

- Tap any cell to focus it (and select its track), then adjust with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> (hold <Key k="FN" /> for steps of 10), or hold the cell to jump directly to a value on the pads. Inactive cells appear dimmed.
- Press <Key k="OK" /> (or tap a focused CHORUS cell again) to step CHORUS modes or toggle other cells between off and their previous value. Press <Key k="FN+OK" /> (or choose **RESET** in the FN menu) to restore the default.
- These controls edit the same `fx.*` parameters found on each track's INSTR FX page (see [Sends: delay and reverb](./effects.mdx#fx-sends)).
