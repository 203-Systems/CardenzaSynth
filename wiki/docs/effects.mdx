# Effects

Each track has its own insert effect chain plus two auxiliary sends feeding shared master delay and reverb buses. The summed output then passes through a master EQ, bus compressor, and tape saturation stage.

```text
engine → DRIVE → CRUSH → FILTER → CHORUS → track fader ─┬─→ out
                                                       ├─ DELAY send ─→ master delay ─┐
                                                       └─ REVERB send → master reverb ┴→ EQ → COMP → TAPE → out
```

Effects are controlled in three places:

## Master effects {#fx-master}

Master bus effects live on <Scene name="TAPE" /> <Page name="FX" /> and save with the project (project format v4 and newer):

<Shot name="tape-fx">
  <Mark at="cells:1,1-1,4">DELAY: tempo-synced tape delay</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:2,1-2,4">REVERB: 4-line FDN reverb</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:3,1">COMP: bus compressor</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,2-3,3">EQ: low/high shelf (±12 dB)</Mark>
  <Mark at="cell:3,4">TAPE: tape saturation</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:4,1-4,4">Tempo-synced echo preview and reverb decay</Mark>
  <Caption>Master FX page.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **DELAY**: **TIME** selects a BPM-synced note division from 1/32 to 1/2 (default 1/8D). The feedback loop includes a **TONE** low-pass filter and an 80 Hz high-pass filter, and changing delay time pitches smoothly like a tape echo. Echoes up to 743 ms allocate a 16 KB buffer; longer times request 32 KB (up to 1.49 s), automatically halving the division if memory is unavailable.
- **REVERB**: dark-voiced 4-line feedback delay network (~4.5 kHz bandwidth).
- Both send buses downsample to 11.025 kHz before processing, giving the delay and reverb a warm, lo-fi character.

<Keywords k="master, bus, tape delay, reverb, eq, compressor, saturation, 总线" />

## Memory and CPU {#fx-cpu}

Effect buffers are allocated on demand and freed when unused: 16 KB for delay (32 KB for times over 743 ms) and ~8.8 KB for reverb in the device's 2 MB PSRAM, plus 1 KB per track for chorus.

- If PSRAM is too low to allocate delay or reverb, that bus stays silent and displays `NO MEM` on the FX page and signal chain. When all effects are bypassed, output is bit-identical to running without effects.
- Polyphony per track is 4 voices for DX7, 6 for JUNO and DRUMS, and 4 for SAMPLER. Exceeding polyphony steals the oldest voice, prioritizing released notes first.
- Audio rendering has a 5.8 ms budget per 256-sample block across both ESP32-S3 cores. Under heavy CPU load, Cardenza smoothly fades out the least audible voices on whichever track has the most voices sounding (released tails first, then the quietest held note). A track never steals voices from another track, and sequencer timing stays locked.

:::warning

Dense chords stacked with multiple effects can still cause audible crackles. Reduce polyphony or bypass an effect or two if you hear dropouts.

:::
