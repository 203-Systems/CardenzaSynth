# Sampling

<Icon name="sample" /> The **SAMPLER** engine (<Track n="4" /> by default) plays WAV files loaded from the SD card or recorded directly with the built-in microphone. All Cardenza apps share the `CardenzaSynth/Samples` folder on the SD card.

**Supported format**: mono 16-bit PCM WAV, 8–96 kHz sample rate, up to 5 minutes long. Samples up to 2 seconds load into RAM; longer files stream directly from the SD card.

<Keywords k="sample, sampling, sampler, microphone, record audio, wav, field recording, vocals, 采样" />

## Loading a sample {#sampler-load}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold TAB + G4, hold CTRL + G4" shot="instr-sampler-sample" at="cell:1,1">In <Scene name="INSTR" />, switch to <Track n="4" /> and open the SAMPLE page (hold <Key k="CTRL" />, press G4).</Step>
  <Step keys="G1" shot="sampler-files" at="0,13,240,10">Tap FILE to open the sample browser. Press <Key k="FN+F" /> to filter by filename.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="instr-sampler-sample" at="cells:1,3-1,4">Select a file with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" />, press <Key k="OK" /> to load, or <Key k="ESC" /> to go back.</Step>
</Strip>

On any page of a SAMPLER track, the FN menu also gives you quick access to **SAMPLES** (file browser), **RECORD** (mic recorder), and **PREVIEW** (audition the sample at its original pitch). Once loaded, the pads play the sample chromatically across the keyboard, with **ROOT** determining which note plays at original pitch.

:::tip

In the web simulator, click **Load WAV...** to import audio files from your computer into the shared sample folder (see [Storage and samples](./web.mdx#web-storage)).

:::

## Recording a sample {#sampler-record}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G2" shot="sampler-rec" at="8,32,222,34">Tap REC on the SAMPLE page (or choose RECORD in the FN menu) to open the recorder. When it shows `READY`, you can record up to 60 seconds.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="sampler-rec" at="8,72,222,12">Press <Key k="OK" /> to start recording and <Key k="OK" /> again to stop. The meter shows live input level.</Step>
</Strip>

- With an SD card inserted, recordings stream directly to `CardenzaSynth/Session/take-<track>.wav` and flush roughly once per second, so a sudden power loss loses at most the last second. If the card fills up, recording stops safely and preserves everything captured so far (`CARD FULL`). Without an SD card, recording uses available RAM and is limited to a few seconds.
- Because the microphone and speaker share a hardware clock pin, the speaker mutes during recording while the sequencer continues running silently.
- Leaving the recorder screen (switching scenes or tracks) or pressing <Key k="ESC" /> during recording cancels the take.
- Unsaved takes survive power cycles and switching apps: the next time Cardenza Synth boots, it opens straight to the recorder with your take intact (`UNSAVED TAKE IS BACK`).

:::warning

Unsaved takes are protected from accidental deletion: starting a new recording prompts `RECORD AGAIN?`, and pressing <Key k="ESC" /> in the trimmer prompts `DISCARD TAKE?` (<Key k="OK" /> to confirm, <Key k="ESC" /> to cancel).

:::

<Keywords k="record sample, microphone, mic, record audio, vocals, take, unsaved take, power cut, 录采样" />

## Trimming and previewing {#sampler-trim}

Stopping a recording automatically opens the sample trimmer:

| Key | Function |
|---|---|
| <Key k="G1" /> / <Key k="G2" /> (or <Key k="OK" />) | Select the start or end marker (active marker lights fully); adjust with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> (hold <Key k="FN" /> for coarse steps) |
| <Key k="G3" /> or <Key k="PLAY" /> | Preview the trimmed region |
| <Key k="G4" /> | **SAVE**: save immediately as the next available `TAKE_nn` file and load it onto the track |

## Saving, renaming, deleting {#sampler-save}

- Pressing **SAVE** (G4) in the trimmer saves the take as a WAV file using the next available name (`TAKE_01`, `TAKE_02`, and so on) without prompting for a filename.
- In the sample browser, press <Key k="FN+OK" /> to **RENAME** or **DELETE** the highlighted file. Deleting prompts for confirmation and removes the WAV file from the SD card.
- Samples currently loaded on any track cannot be deleted (`FILE IN USE`).
- Sample files live in the shared `Samples/` folder rather than inside individual project files; projects reference samples by filename.
