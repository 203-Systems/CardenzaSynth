# 1 INSTR

**INSTR** controls the active track's sound: engine selection, preset browsing, MIDI effects (chord and arpeggiator), synth parameters, and track effects. Each of the four tracks runs its own engine and remembers which INSTR page you last viewed.

| Engine | Default track | Pages (<Key k="CTRL" /> steps through them) |
|---|---|---|
| DRUMS (808 drum machine) | <Track n="1" /> | VOICE, KIT, FX |
| JUNO (analog synth) | <Track n="2" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, DCO, VCF, ENV, LFO, FX |
| DX7 (FM synth) | <Track n="3" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, ALGO, LEVELS, OP1–OP6, LFO, FX |
| SAMPLER | <Track n="4" /> | VOICE, CHORD, ARP, SAMPLE, TONE, FX |

Pages follow signal flow: **VOICE** (engine and preset) → MIDI effects (**CHORD**, **ARP**) → synth/sampler parameters → track **FX**. When a track has more pages than fit across the bottom bar, `<` and `>` indicators appear with a `page / total` counter on the right. Hold <Key k="CTRL" /> anytime to jump directly to a page using the page map.

VOICE is the landing page for every engine. It shows the current preset name in large type above a live keyboard (or eight velocity pads on a drum track).

- Press <Key k="ADD" /> or <Key k="SUB" /> to load the previous or next preset directly (ADD moves up the list, matching the browser). Hold <Key k="FN" /> with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to jump 10 presets at a time.
- Press <Key k="OK" /> to open the preset browser.
- You can also access **PRESETS**, **SAVE**, **SAVE AS**, and **ENGINE** from the FN menu on any INSTR page without returning to VOICE (see [Menus by screen](./fn.mdx#fn-menus)).

Switching a track's engine loads the new engine's default preset and returns to VOICE. Your patterns, chord settings, and arpeggiator settings stay intact.

## Browsing and saving sounds {#instr-presets}

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G2" shot="preset-browser" at="0,13,240,10">Tap PRESET (or press <Key k="OK" /> on VOICE) to open the preset list, with your position and total count at the top right.</Step>
  <Step keys="FN + F, B, A, S, S, OK" shot="preset-filtered" at="0,13,240,10">Press <Key k="FN+F" /> to filter by name (for example, `BASS`) and press <Key k="OK" /> to show only matching presets.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="instr-voice" at="8,40,200,26">Scroll with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> and press <Key k="OK" /> to load.</Step>
</Strip>

- Inside the preset list, the pads and GRID do not type, preventing accidental filtering. Always press <Key k="FN+F" /> to start a search, and submit an empty search to restore the full list.
- Press <Key k="ESC" /> once to clear an active search filter, or again to close the browser.
- Scroll with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> (or <Key k="FN" /> + <Key k=";" /> / <Key k="." />). Hold <Key k="FN" /> with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to jump 8 rows at a time.
- **SAVE** (in the FN menu) overwrites your own user presets directly. If you edited a factory preset, SAVE creates a new numbered user preset automatically (for example, `LEAD I 2`). Use **SAVE AS** to enter a custom name. User presets are stored on the SD card by engine under `CardenzaSynth/Presets/<engine>/`.

<Keywords k="presets, preset, preset library, browse, search, search sound, find, filter, fn f, save sound, save as, patch library, 音色库" />

## Parameter pages {#instr-params}

Engine pages (as well as CHORD and ARP) map parameters directly to the 4×4 GRID. Tap a GRID key to focus its parameter, then adjust it with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> (hold to auto-repeat, or hold <Key k="FN" /> for coarse steps). Press <Key k="FN+OK" /> (or choose **RESET** in the FN menu) to restore a parameter's default value. For switches and multi-choice parameters, tapping the focused GRID key again (or pressing <Key k="OK" />) cycles through options.

Unused cells below the parameters display live visualizations such as envelopes, filter curves, FM algorithm diagrams, waveforms, or an oscilloscope. See the engine tables below for every parameter's range, options, and default value.

**Quick value picking**: Hold any parameter cell on the GRID and the 32 pads temporarily map across that parameter's range (or options), with the current value fully lit. Tap any pad to jump straight to that value, then release the GRID key to return the pads to normal playing. A quick tap still just selects the parameter.

## CHORD and ARP pages {#instr-midifx}

Right after VOICE, every melodic track has its own **CHORD** page (single-key chords) and **ARP** page (arpeggiator). Both belong to the track rather than the preset, so they stay active when you change sounds or engines and are saved with the project. Drum tracks do not have CHORD or ARP pages. For full details, see [Chords](./play.mdx#play-chords) and [Arpeggiator](./play.mdx#play-arp).

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G2" shot="instr-chord" at="cells:1,1-1,2">CHORD sets chord mode and voicing, and previews both the root chord and every diatonic triad in the current scale.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold CTRL + G3" shot="instr-arp" at="cells:1,1-2,1">ARP configures mode, RATE, RANGE, GATE, and HOLD with a live step-order display below.</Step>
</Strip>

Classic Juno-style subtractive synthesis: DCO oscillator → VCF filter → VCA envelope, plus an LFO and chorus.

## SAMPLER {#engine-sampler}

Plays WAV audio files loaded from the SD card or recorded through the built-in microphone. The **SAMPLE** page handles file loading, recording, start/end trimming, root note, tuning, looping, and gain alongside a waveform view. **TONE** provides filter, attack/release envelope, and level controls. See [Sampling](./sampler.mdx) for the full workflow.
