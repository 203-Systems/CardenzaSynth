## No sound {#faq-no-sound}

Check these items in order:

  <Step shot="tape-mix" at="cells:2,1-3,4">Check <Scene name="TAPE" /> <Page name="MIX" />: make sure the track is not muted (`MUTE` lit), no other track is soloed (`SOLO` lit), and the track fader is up.</Step>
  <Step shot="instr-juno-fx" at="cells:1,1-1,3">Check <Scene name="INSTR" /> <Page name="FX" />: verify that FILTER is not swept all the way to one extreme and CRUSH/DRIVE are not choking the signal.</Step>

- On a SAMPLER track, make sure a WAV sample is loaded (see [Loading a sample](./sampler.mdx#sampler-load)).
- During microphone sampling, the speaker is muted due to a shared hardware clock and resumes once you exit the recorder.
- In the web simulator, click anywhere on the page first to unlock browser audio (see [Sound](./web.mdx#web-audio)).
- Still silent? Press <Key k="FN+ESC" /> (All Stop) and try again.

## A note keeps sounding {#faq-stuck}

Press <Key k="FN+ESC" /> (All Stop) to halt the sequencer, turn off all latched arpeggiators, and silence every sounding note. (The side **HOME** button opens the system menu rather than stopping playback.)

<Keywords k="stuck note, hanging note, won't stop, drone, panic, all notes off, latch, hold, 卡音" />

## Why are the lights behaving like this? {#faq-lights}

Key LEDs use four states: <Led state="off">off</Led>, <Led state="half">half lit</Led>, <Led state="full">fully lit</Led>, and <Led state="blink">blinking</Led> (see [Off, half lit, fully lit, blinking](./lights.mdx#lights-states)). While holding <Key k="FN" />, only keys with an active FN shortcut light up; all others stay dark and disabled. If every lit key looks equally bright, focus **KEY LED** on <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="SYSTEM" /> to inspect the 8-column brightness ramp on the pads (see [Brightness and the light test](./lights.mdx#lights-ramp)): each column should step visibly brighter from left to right.

<Keywords k="lights wrong, all lit, too bright, too dim, dark keys, 灯不对" />

## Where did the FN panel go? {#faq-settings}

<Key k="FN" /> now opens a context menu for the current screen (see [The FN menu](./fn.mdx#fn-menu)), while quick controls (<Key k="FN" /> + <Key k="6" /> / <Key k="7" /> / <Key k="8" />) and letter shortcuts work as usual. Settings previously in the FN panel moved to dedicated pages:

- **Keyboard and device settings** (layout, scale, root, octave, velocity, lock, SELECT ONE/MULTI, volume, key LEDs, screen brightness): <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="KEYS" /> and <Page name="SYSTEM" />. Hold <Key k="ESC" /> and press column 1 on the GRID, or pick **SETUP** at the bottom right of any FN menu (see [0 SETUP](./setup.mdx)).
- **BPM, swing, and PLAY (LOOP/SONG)**: <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="PROJECT" />.
- **Chord and arpeggiator**: per-track **CHORD** and **ARP** pages in <Scene name="INSTR" /> on melodic tracks (see [Chords](./play.mdx#play-chords)).
- **Pattern step length**: <Scene name="SEQ" /> <Page name="TRACK" /> (see [TRACK page](./seq.mdx#seq-track)).

<Keywords k="fn panel, where are the settings, settings moved, setup, preferences, fn 1, fn 5, chord, arp, swing, 设置在哪" />

## Where is my work saved? Can I just unplug power? {#faq-save}

Yes, as long as an SD card is installed. Your active session (patterns, presets, mixer, song arrangement, keyboard, chords, arpeggiators, and tempo) autosaves 1.5 seconds after you stop pressing keys (or at least once per minute during continuous use) and reloads when you power back on. Retrospective capture takes and play/stop state are not included in autosave.

To save a permanent project file, press <Key k="FN+S" /> (auto-names untitled projects as `SONG 01`, `SONG 02`, etc.) or use **SAVE AS** from the FN menu on <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="PROJECT" /> (see [Save, save as, new, open](./projects.mdx#project-save)). Projects are saved in `CardenzaSynth/Projects` on the SD card, and device settings autosave automatically.

:::warning

Without an SD card, nothing is saved. Cardenza displays `NO SD CARD, NOTHING WILL BE SAVED` at boot, and all work is lost on power-off. Cards inserted after boot require a restart to mount (see [No card or card errors](./projects.mdx#storage-card)).

:::

<Keywords k="lost my work, where saved, autosave, power off, pull the plug, battery, switch off, save name, song 01, 丢失, 断电" />

## Is there undo? {#faq-undo}

Cardenza Synth does not have undo. Destructive actions like clearing a full pattern, overwriting a file, or creating a new project always ask for confirmation first (<Key k="OK" /> to proceed, <Key k="ESC" /> to cancel).

:::tip

Before making major changes, save a snapshot under a new name (**SAVE AS** in SETUP PROJECT's FN menu) or duplicate the current pattern (<Key k="FN+D" />). You can also recover improvised phrases from retrospective capture (see [Capture: recover what you just played](./seq.mdx#capture)).

:::

<Keywords k="undo, redo, revert, go back, 撤销" />

## Can I use USB MIDI or USB audio with a computer? {#faq-midi}

Yes. Over a single USB cable, Cardenza acts as both a class-compliant USB MIDI device and a USB audio input (no drivers required):

- Appears on your computer as **203 Systems Cardenza**.
- **MIDI in**: external keyboards or DAWs can play tracks 1–4 on MIDI channels 1–4 (any other channel plays the active track). CC messages route to the target track's synth engine. CC 120/123 (All Sound Off / All Notes Off) and unplugging USB both release any computer-triggered notes.
- **MIDI out**: live pad performance and sequencer playback transmit over USB MIDI on each track's corresponding channel (track 1 on channel 1, etc.).
- **USB audio**: your computer can record Cardenza's audio output directly (pre-master-volume, identical on L/R). Audio currently runs device-to-computer only; computer playback is not routed back to Cardenza's speaker.

Hardware MIDI jacks and MIDI clock sync are not currently supported. The same USB connection also powers interactive lessons in the [guide](/) (see [Connect your Cardenza (USB)](./web.mdx#web-usb)).

<Keywords k="midi, usb midi, usb audio, sound card, audio interface, computer, daw, external keyboard, sync, clock, 外接键盘, 电脑" />

## How do long patterns work? {#faq-long}

Patterns support 1 to 256 steps, shown 16 steps at a time on the GRID with a page counter at the bottom right (such as `2/4`):

- **Set length**: adjust **STEPS** on <Scene name="SEQ" /> <Page name="TRACK" />, or use **LEN − / LEN +** in the SEQ FN menu to step by full pages.
- **Flip pages**: press <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> on the note page with no steps selected, or use **PAGE − / PAGE +** in the FN menu. During playback, the GRID follows the playhead automatically.
- **Switch patterns**: press <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> on the note page to step to the next or previous pattern.

Every pattern stores its own independent step length (see [TRACK page](./seq.mdx#seq-track)).

<Keywords k="long pattern, more than 16 steps, 32 steps, 64 steps, pattern length, page, steps, 长 pattern" />

## How do I search for a preset by name? {#faq-search}

Open the preset browser (<Key k="OK" /> on <Scene name="INSTR" /> <Page name="VOICE" />, or **PRESETS** in the FN menu), press <Key k="FN+F" />, type part of the name, and press <Key k="OK" />. Pads are muted inside lists so accidental taps won't trigger a filter. The sample browser works the same way (see [Browsing and saving sounds](./instr.mdx#instr-presets)).

<Keywords k="search, find preset, find sample, filter, by name, 搜索" />

## How do I switch to another app? {#faq-apps}

Cardenza Synth is one firmware app managed by the 203 Launcher. Press the side <Key k="HOME" /> button and choose **Back to Launcher** (on firmware older than 0.2.1, hold <Key k="FN" /> while plugging in USB), then open **My Apps** and select another firmware with <Key k="OK" />.

You can browse and install additional firmware from the [203 Community](https://stage.203.io/) using **Install over USB** (Chrome or Edge, no account needed) or directly over Wi-Fi through the Launcher's **Store**. With an SD card installed, your working session saves automatically before switching apps and restores when you return.

<Keywords k="app, launcher, switch app, other firmware, install firmware, update firmware, community, 应用, 启动器, 安装固件" />

## Crackles while playing {#faq-crackle}

Audio DSP headroom is tight; dense chords combined with multiple effects can exceed the per-block CPU budget. Play fewer simultaneous notes, turn off one or two effects (especially multiple choruses or heavy reverb sends), or lower the arpeggiator rate. See [Memory and CPU](./effects.mdx#fx-cpu).

<Keywords k="crackle, glitch, pops, distortion, audio dropout, 爆音" />
