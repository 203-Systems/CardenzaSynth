# 2 SEQ

Every track has up to 16 patterns. Each pattern has its own length, 1–256 steps (16 to start with), and the GRID shows it one page of 16 steps at a time. Each step holds up to 8 notes (a chord), each note with its own length, velocity and a small timing offset (6 microsteps per step). SEQ has three pages:

| Page | For |
|---|---|
| <Page name="ROLL" /> / <Page name="STEP" /> note page | writing, editing and recording notes, in two views: ROLL piano roll (default) and STEP step recording; on a drum track it is the <Page name="DRUMS" /> drum machine |
| <Page name="PATTERN" /> | choosing, creating and queueing the track's patterns |
| <Page name="TRACK" /> | the track's sequencer switch, the current pattern's length, global swing and BPM |

<Keywords k="sequencer, sequence, pattern, steps, loop, beat, arrangement, 音序器" />

## The note page's two views {#seq-notes}

<Key k="OK" /> is this page's <Icon name="rec" /> **record key**: tap it to switch between the two views, hold it for the RECORD panel. With steps selected a tap on <Key k="OK" /> picks what you edit instead (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).

<Strip>
  <Step shot="seq-notes" at="footer"><Icon name="roll" /> **ROLL piano roll** (default): time × pitch, notes drawn as bars.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-step-view" at="footer"><Icon name="step" /> Tap <Key k="OK" />: **STEP recording**, a 4×4 of steps with note names.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-notes" at="footer">Tap again: back to ROLL. The OK strip on screen is <Led state="half" /> in ROLL, <Led state="full" /> in STEP.</Step>
</Strip>

Both views edit the same pattern, and the 16 GRID keys are the 16 steps of the page on screen in both (row 1 is steps 1–4, row 2 steps 5–8…).

|  | <Icon name="roll" /> ROLL | <Icon name="step" /> STEP |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | time × pitch, notes as bars | a 4×4 of steps with note names or chords |
| GRID tap | goes to that step (tap it again to let go); with SELECT MULTI it adds steps | moves the write cursor to the step |
| Playing PADS | add / remove notes on selected steps; with none selected they just play (and record live with REC on) | write into the cursor step, the cursor moves on when released |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | change the selected steps; with none selected, next / previous pattern | move the cursor one step |
| <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> | bigger steps on the selection; with none selected, next / previous page of a long pattern | move the cursor a page (4 steps in a one-page pattern) |

<Keywords k="view, mode, piano roll, step view, switch view, 视图" />

The page's 16 steps run left to right, pitch bottom to top (the range around the notes in use, shown automatically: at least an octave, black-key rows shaded). The more solid a note, the higher its velocity; the green line is the playhead. The small cells at the bottom: dithered where a step holds notes, white where it is selected, green where the playhead is. An empty pattern says HOLD A PAD + TAP STEPS.

**Write notes: hold one or more PADS, then tap steps on the GRID.**

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold r2c1~C" shot="seq-hold-pad">Hold a note (C).</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r2c1~C + G1" shot="seq-hold-tap" at="28,57,15,10">Keep holding and tap GRID 1: C is written into step 1.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold r2c1~C + G5, G9" shot="seq-notes" at="28,110,195,11">Go on with 5, 9…, then switch to another note and tap a few more steps.</Step>
</Strip>

- **Go to a step**: tap a GRID key to go to that step; its notes light up fully on the pads, so you see at once whether the step has notes. Tap the same step again to let go. See [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit).
- **Record live**: turn REC on (<Key k="FN+PLAY" /> or the RECORD panel), start playback, then play the pads, see [Play and live recording](#seq-transport).
- With the track's sequencer off (TRACK page) the page says SEQUENCE OFF.

- A press replaces the notes the cursor step had.

- While playing, the step the playhead enters flashes green once and keeps a green outline until the next step; the steps behind it leave no trail.
- VIEW in the FN menu switches the view too, from every SEQ page.

## Selecting and editing {#seq-edit}

In the ROLL view a GRID tap **goes to a step**: only that step is selected, tapping another step moves there, and tapping the same step again lets go (the pads play again). The notes of the step you are on light up fully on the pads; on a step without notes the pads stay a dim keyboard. To select several steps at once, set **SELECT** to MULTI on the KEYS page of <Scene name="SETUP" /> (see [KEYS](./setup.mdx#setup-keys)).

<Shot name="seq-selected">
  <Mark at="76,15,14,96">The step you are on</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="G5">Current step: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="r2c1">This step's note: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="PADS">Other keys: half lit</KeyMark>
  <Caption>On step 5: its column is highlighted and its note lights up fully on the pads.</Caption>
</Shot>

While playing with no step selected, the notes of the step under the playhead light up on the pads too. Once on a step:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G5" shot="seq-selected" at="76,15,14,96">Tap GRID 5: step 5 is selected and its column highlighted.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK, OK" shot="seq-lane" at="footer"><Key k="OK" /> cycles what you edit, **NOTE → LEN → VEL → TIME**; the bottom line shows the lane and its value.</Step>
  <Step keys="ADD" shot="seq-lane" at="footer"><Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> change it; held they repeat. <Key k="ESC" /> clears the selection.</Step>
</Strip>

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| PADS | add or remove that note on the selected steps (each tone of a chord separately); while playing they edit without sounding |
| <Key k="OK" /> | what you edit: NOTE (pitch) → LEN (length) → VEL (velocity) → TIME (small timing offset) |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | change it; held they repeat; with <Key k="FN" /> in bigger steps |
| <Key k="FN+OK" /> | switch between editing all notes of the chord (ALL) and just one of them (its name on the bottom line) |
| <Key k="ESC" /> | clear the selection (the next ESC changes scene) |

While steps are selected, their notes are <Led state="full">fully lit</Led> on the pads.

## DRUMS: the drum machine page {#seq-drums}

On a drum track (<Track n="1" /> by default) the note page is <Page name="DRUMS" />, a drum machine, instead of ROLL / STEP.

<Shot name="seq-drums">
  <Mark at="9,15,95,108">Eight drums × 16 steps</Mark>
  <Mark at="106,15,125,108">The chosen drum on the GRID</Mark>
  <Mark at="footer">DRUMS page</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="G1 G3 G5">The chosen drum (CH) hits: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="G2">Nothing on this step: off</KeyMark>
  <Caption>DRUMS on T1: the beat of every drum on the left, the chosen drum's 16 steps on the right.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **PADS** choose the drum and how hard: each column is a drum, the rows are how hard (top row hardest). The pad sounds and the GRID now writes that drum at that velocity.
- **GRID**: a tap on an empty step adds a hit at once (stopped, you hear it); a tap on an existing hit takes it out when you let go.
- **Hold a hit**: the screen shows its value, <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> change its velocity; <Key k="OK" /> switches to its timing within the step (6 microsteps) and back. An edited or long-held hit stays.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="hold G1" shot="seq-drums" at="110,26,28,24">Hold the CH hit on step 1 (keep holding).</Step>
  <Step keys="hold G1 + ADD" shot="seq-drum-edit" at="12,96,213,25">Press <Key k="ADD" />: the hit gets harder and the screen shows CH VELOCITY. Let go and the hit stays.</Step>
</Strip>

- GRID lights: the chosen drum's hits fully lit, steps with only other drums half lit, empty steps off.
- There is no ROLL / STEP view on a drum track; record live and the RECORD panel work as on the other tracks.

- REC can also be turned on in the RECORD panel or with REC in the FN menu. While recording is armed the PLAY strip on screen is red.
- Recording is overdub: existing notes stay, and new ones land on the nearest microstep. With ARP on, the arpeggiator's notes are what gets recorded.
- Live recording works in the ROLL view and on the DRUMS page with no step selected; in the STEP view the pads write into the cursor step instead.
- A wrong note: select its step and clear it with <Key k="FN+X" />, or tidy the timing with <Key k="FN+Q" />.

| GRID | Holds |
|---|---|
| row 1 | **REC** live recording on / off · **SNAP** whether takes land on whole steps (STEPS) or keep their timing (FREE) · **STEP** how far step recording moves (1 / 2 / 4) · **VIEW** ROLL / STEP |

- Setting cells: a tap switches them; <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> work too.
- Take cells: a tap chooses; <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> choose the bar; <Key k="OK" /> puts it into a new pattern; <Key k="FN+OK" /> merges it into the current pattern.
- GRID lights: settings half lit, REC and SNAP fully lit when on, the focused cell fully lit, takes half lit.

## Capture: get back what you just played {#capture}

<Icon name="capture" /> No need to turn on REC: every note you play on the pads is kept in the background (capture). When something good came out, put it into the sequencer afterwards:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="r2c1~C, r2c3~E, r2c5~G" shot="seq-keyboard">Just play. A pause longer than 2.5 s, a track change or 60 seconds starts a new take.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold OK" shot="record" at="cells:2,1-3,4">Hold <Key k="OK" />: the two middle rows are the last 8 takes, with number, track and note count.</Step>
  <Step keys="G6" shot="record" at="cell:2,2 cells:4,1-4,4">Tap a take: row 4 shows how its bar would land. <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> pick another bar.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="record-placed" at="14,94,212,28"><Key k="OK" />: into a **new pattern** on this track; not a single existing note is touched.</Step>
</Strip>

- The 8 takes share room for 400 notes; when it is full, the oldest take makes way.
- Without a free pattern slot it tells you; use <Key k="FN+OK" /> to merge into the current pattern instead.
- Takes played while the transport ran keep their place against the bar; takes played while stopped start with their first note, timed at the BPM of that moment.
- With SNAP on (default) every note lands on the nearest whole step with a whole-step length; off, your playing's fine timing is kept.

## Long patterns and step pages {#seq-long}

A pattern can be up to 256 steps long; the GRID and the screen always show one page of 16 steps. The page number sits at the right end of the bottom line (for example `2/4`, yellow when you turned the page yourself).

<Shot name="fn-held">
  <Mark at="cells:3,1-3,2">PAGE − / PAGE +: page / pages</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,3-3,4">LEN − / LEN +: the length</Mark>
  <Caption>The SEQ menu (hold FN): row 3 turns the pages and sets the length.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **Set the length**: STEPS on the TRACK page (<Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> one step, <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> to the next page boundary), or LEN − / LEN + in the FN menu: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 48 … 256.
- **Turn the page**: <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> on the note page with no step selected, or PAGE − / PAGE + in the FN menu.
- While playing the GRID follows the playhead from page to page; after you turn a page yourself it stays there until the next PLAY. With a step selected it does not turn.
- A new pattern starts with the length of the current one; a duplicated pattern keeps its length. Steps beyond the length are off on the GRID.
- Memory is used only by pages that hold notes. When it runs out the note is not written and the screen says MEMORY FULL.

- Tap a slot: switch to that pattern. Stopped, it switches at once; playing, it is **queued** until the track finishes its loop (the cell shows NEXT and a countdown bar, its key light is <Led state="blink">blinking</Led>).
- Tap the "NEW" slot: a new empty pattern (up to 16 per track).
- The pattern in use shows how many of its steps hold notes.
- <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" />: next / previous (queued while playing); <Key k="OK" /> goes back to the note page.
- <Key k="FN+D" />: duplicate the current pattern into the next free slot. <Key k="FN+C" /> / <Key k="FN+V" /> copy and paste a whole pattern, <Key k="FN+X" /> clears it (after confirming).

## Copy, paste, clear, quantize {#seq-tools}

Hold <Key k="FN" /> with a letter, or pick the item in the FN menu (COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, QUANT, DUP: their second line says STEPS or PATTERN). On the note page they act on the selected steps; with none selected, and on PATTERN and TRACK, on the whole pattern:

<Keymap>
  <Group keys="FN">Hold</Group>
  <Group keys="C">Copy</Group>
  <Group keys="V">Paste</Group>
  <Group keys="X">Clear</Group>
  <Group keys="Q">Quantize</Group>
  <Group keys="D">Duplicate pattern</Group>
  <Group keys="T">Tap tempo</Group>
  <Caption>Edit keys while FN is held</Caption>
</Keymap>

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| <Key k="FN+C" /> / <Key k="FN+V" /> | copy / paste (PASTE is off until something is copied) |
| <Key k="FN+X" /> | clear (asks first for a whole pattern) |
| <Key k="FN+Q" /> | quantize to whole steps |
| <Key k="FN+D" /> | duplicate the pattern into the next free slot |
| <Key k="FN+T" /> | tap tempo |

The SEQ menu on the GRID, the same on all three pages:

| Row | Items |
|---|---|
| 1 | COPY · PASTE · CLEAR · QUANT |
| 2 | DUP · VIEW (ROLL / STEP) · RECORD (the RECORD panel) · SCOPE (<Key k="FN+OK" />, with steps selected) |
| 3 | PAGE − · PAGE + (note page, long patterns) · LEN − · LEN + |
| 4 | SAVE · TAP · REC · SETUP, as on every screen |

More on the menu in [The menu on each screen](./fn.mdx#fn-menus).

:::warning

There is no undo, so clearing a whole pattern always asks first.

:::
