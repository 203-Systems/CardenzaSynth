# 2 SEQ

Each of the four tracks holds up to 16 patterns. Every pattern has its own length from 1 to 256 steps (16 by default), displayed on the GRID one 16-step page at a time. Each step can store up to 8 simultaneous notes, with independent length, velocity, and microtiming offset (6 microsteps per step).

**SEQ** has three pages:

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| <Page name="ROLL" /> / <Page name="STEP" /> (note page) | Enter, edit, and record notes in either piano roll (ROLL) or step-grid (STEP) view; on drum tracks, STEP is the <Page name="DRUMS" /> drum machine |
| <Page name="PATTERN" /> | Select, create, and queue patterns for the current track |
| <Page name="TRACK" /> | Toggle the track sequencer on/off, set pattern length, and adjust global swing and BPM |

<Keywords k="sequencer, sequence, pattern, steps, loop, beat, arrangement, 音序器" />

## The note page's two views {#seq-notes}

On the note page, <Key k="OK" /> serves as the <Icon name="rec" /> **record/view key**: tap it to toggle between ROLL and STEP views, or hold it to open the RECORD panel. When one or more steps are selected, tapping <Key k="OK" /> cycles through the step edit lanes instead (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).

<Strip>
  <Step shot="seq-notes" at="footer"><Icon name="roll" /> **ROLL (piano roll)**: time runs left to right and pitch bottom to top, showing notes as horizontal bars. Melodic tracks default to ROLL.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-step-view" at="footer 116,14,113,108"><Icon name="step" /> Tap <Key k="OK" /> for **STEP view**: every pitch gets a row on the left, while the right shows the 4×4 GRID for the last pad you pressed. Drum tracks default to STEP (labeled DRUMS).</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-notes" at="footer">Tap <Key k="OK" /> again to return to ROLL. The OK strip on screen is <Led state="half">half lit</Led> in ROLL and <Led state="full">fully lit</Led> in STEP.</Step>
</Strip>

Both views edit the same underlying pattern and use identical controls; only the visual display changes. Drum tracks and melodic tracks remember their preferred view separately. The 16 GRID keys map to the 16 steps of the current page (row 1 = steps 1–4, row 2 = steps 5–8, and so on):

| Input | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Tap GRID | Select and latch that step so both hands are free for the pads. Tap it again to deselect, or tap another step to move the selection (with `SELECT MULTI`, each tap toggles steps in or out of the selection). |
| Hold GRID | Temporarily select the step while held; hold multiple GRID keys to edit several steps together. When playback is stopped, holding a step previews its notes. |
| Hold PADS + tap GRID | Toggle the held note(s) on that step (adds them if absent, or removes them if any are already on that step). |
| Press PADS (with steps selected) | Toggle that pad's note (or full chord) on all selected steps. |
| Press PADS (no steps selected) | Play the track live (and record into the pattern if REC is armed). |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | Adjust the active edit lane on selected steps; with no steps selected, switch to the next/previous 16-step page on long patterns. |
| <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> | Coarse adjustments on selected steps; with no steps selected, switch to the next/previous pattern. |
| <Key k="OK" /> | Tap to toggle ROLL/STEP (or cycle edit lanes when steps are selected); hold 0.45 s to open the RECORD panel. |

There is no auto-advancing write cursor; any step you leave empty plays as a rest.

<Keywords k="view, mode, piano roll, step view, switch view, step keys, matrixos, 视图" />

Steps run left to right (patterns shorter than 16 steps stretch to fill the width) and pitch runs bottom to top. On melodic tracks, rows cover every note playable at the current octave with black-key rows shaded, expanding automatically up to 41 rows if notes exist outside that range. On drum tracks, rows display the active drums by name. Denser shading indicates higher velocity, and the green vertical line is the playhead. Along the bottom strip, dithered cells contain notes, white cells are selected, and green marks the playhead.

While you hold pads, the corresponding pitch rows (including chord tones) highlight across the roll so you can see exactly where notes will land. An empty pattern prompts `HOLD A PAD + TAP STEPS`.

**To enter notes, hold one or more PADS and tap steps on the GRID:**

- **Inspect a step**: tap its GRID key to light up all of its notes on the pads. Tap the same GRID key again to deselect (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).
- **Live recording**: arm REC (<Key k="FN+PLAY" /> or the RECORD panel), press <Key k="PLAY" />, and play the pads (see [Play and live recording](#seq-transport)).
- If the track's sequencer is disabled on the TRACK page, the screen displays `SEQUENCE OFF - SEQ > TRACK`.

- **Left panel**: one row per pitch across the current 16-step page (same octave range and black-key shading as ROLL, up to 41 rows). Held notes highlight their row; selected steps highlight their column.
- **Right panel**: a 4×4 grid matching the physical GRID for the most recently pressed pad (shown by name at the top). Dither density shows velocity, and selected steps invert.
- All controls match ROLL view (see [The note page's two views](#seq-notes)). To program steps one at a time without keeping tempo:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="seq-step-view" at="116,14,113,108">Tap <Key k="OK" /> to switch to STEP view.</Step>
  <Step keys="G2, r3c2~D, r3c3~E" shot="seq-step" at="144,25,26,22">Tap GRID 2 to latch step 2, then press D and E to add both notes. Pressing E again removes it.</Step>
  <Step keys="G2">Tap GRID 2 again to deselect it, or tap the next step you want to edit.</Step>

- Melodic notes entered from the pads default to one step in length at the SETUP KEYS velocity (`VELO`); drum hits use the velocity of the pad row you pressed. You can adjust note length, velocity, pitch, and microtiming afterward with the edit lanes (see [Selecting and editing](#seq-edit)).
- During playback, the active step flashes green on entry and keeps a green outline until the next step.
- You can also switch between ROLL and STEP using **VIEW** in the FN menu or RECORD panel.

## Selecting and editing {#seq-edit}

In both views and on drum tracks, GRID keys select steps in two ways:

- **Tap** (release within 0.25 s without pressing another key): latches the step selected so your hands are free to use the pads. With **SELECT ONE** (default), tapping a different step moves the selection, and tapping the currently selected step deselects it. With **SELECT MULTI** (configured on <Scene name="SETUP" /> <Page name="KEYS" />, see [KEYS](./setup.mdx#setup-keys)), each tap adds or removes a step from the selection.
- **Hold** (longer than 0.25 s, or while pressing another key): keeps the step selected only while held. Hold multiple GRID keys simultaneously to edit them together.
- Press <Key k="ESC" /> to clear the selection. Changing step pages in SELECT ONE mode also clears it.

<Shot name="seq-selected">

  <KeyMark keys="G5">Selected step: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="r3c1">Note on this step: fully lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="PADS">Other playable pads: half lit</KeyMark>
  <Caption>Step 5 selected: its column is highlighted and its note lights up fully on the pads.</Caption>
</Shot>

When steps are selected, their notes light up at full brightness on the pads. During playback with nothing selected, the pads light up in real time with the currently playing step's notes.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G5" shot="seq-selected" at="76,15,14,96">Tap GRID 5 to select step 5 and highlight its column.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK, OK" shot="seq-lane" at="footer">Tap <Key k="OK" /> to cycle edit lanes: **NOTE → LEN → VEL → TIME**. The active lane and value appear in the footer.</Step>
  <Step keys="ADD" shot="seq-lane" at="footer">Use <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to edit the value (hold to repeat), then press <Key k="ESC" /> to deselect.</Step>
</Strip>

| Key | Function with steps selected |
|---|---|
| PADS | Toggle that pad's note (or full chord) on the selected steps. If a step already contains any note of a chord, pressing the pad removes those notes. During playback, pads edit silently without triggering audio. |
| <Key k="OK" /> | Cycle edit lanes: **NOTE** (pitch) → **LEN** (duration) → **VEL** (velocity) → **TIME** (microtiming offset) |
| <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> | Adjust the current lane (hold to repeat; hold <Key k="FN" /> for coarse steps) |
| <Key k="FN+OK" /> | Toggle chord editing scope between all notes on the step (`ALL`) and individual notes (shown by name in the footer) |
| <Key k="ESC" /> | Clear step selection (press again to switch scenes) |

On drum tracks, pads select which drum to place, so the NOTE lane does not transpose drum hits (pressing ADD/SUB displays `PADS PICK DRUMS`). LEN, VEL, and TIME work normally.

## DRUMS: the drum machine {#seq-drums}

On drum tracks (<Track n="1" /> by default), STEP view is labeled <Page name="DRUMS" /> and serves as the default drum sequencer. Tap <Key k="OK" /> anytime to switch to ROLL view if you prefer a horizontal overview of active drums.

  <Mark at="footer">DRUMS tab</Mark>
  <KeyMark keys="G1 G3 G5">Steps with hits: half lit</KeyMark>
  <KeyMark keys="G2">Empty step: off</KeyMark>
  <Caption>Drum sequencer on T1: all eight drums on the left, and the selected drum's 16 steps on the right.</Caption>

- **PADS**: each column selects and triggers one drum, and each row sets hit velocity (127 at top, then 100, 70, and 40 at bottom). Pressing a pad also focuses that drum in the 4×4 GRID panel on the right.
- **Hold a pad and tap steps**: toggle that drum on the tapped steps at the held row's velocity. You can also select a step first and tap drum pads into it.
- **Edit hits**: select a step (tap or hold), press <Key k="OK" /> to select VEL or TIME, and adjust with <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" />.
- Hit velocity renders in four distinct dither levels (solid, half, quarter, eighth); microtimed off-grid hits appear as a thin bar positioned within the step.
- Holding a pad highlights its drum row across the grid at that row's velocity density and inverts the drum label.

<Strip>

</Strip>

- GRID LEDs follow the standard note-page rules: hits half lit, selected steps fully lit, empty steps off, and matching steps fully lit while holding pads.
- Live recording and the RECORD panel work identically to melodic tracks.

## Capture: recover what you just played {#capture}

<Icon name="capture" /> Cardenza continuously buffers everything you play on the pads in the background, even when REC is off. If you improvise a phrase you want to keep, drop it into a pattern afterward:

<Strip>
  <Step keys="r3c1~C, r3c3~E, r3c5~G" shot="seq-keyboard">Just play freely. A pause longer than 2.5 seconds, switching tracks, or playing for 60 seconds automatically starts a new take.</Step>
  <Step keys="hold OK" shot="record" at="cells:2,1-3,4">Hold <Key k="OK" /> to open the RECORD panel. Rows 2 and 3 list your last 8 takes with take number, track, and note count.</Step>
  <Step keys="G6" shot="record" at="cell:2,2 cells:4,1-4,4">Tap a take to preview its bar on row 4, and use <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to choose another bar if the take spans multiple bars.</Step>
  <Step keys="OK" shot="record-placed" at="14,94,212,28">Press <Key k="OK" /> to drop the bar into a **new pattern** on this track without altering any existing patterns.</Step>
</Strip>

- All 8 takes share a 400-note ring buffer; when full, the oldest take is dropped to make room.
- If all 16 pattern slots are full, Cardenza warns you; press <Key k="FN+OK" /> to merge the take into the current pattern instead.
- Takes played while the sequencer is running preserve their bar alignment. Takes played while stopped align to step 1 from your first note using the current BPM.
- With **SNAP** enabled (default), notes quantize to the nearest whole step with whole-step durations; with SNAP off, original microtiming is preserved.

## Long patterns and step pages {#seq-long}

Patterns can be up to 256 steps long, viewed one 16-step page at a time on the GRID and display. The current page indicator sits at the bottom right of the screen (for example, `2/4`; it turns yellow when you turn pages manually).

<Shot name="fn-held">
  <Mark at="cells:3,1-3,2">PAGE − / PAGE +: switch step pages</Mark>
  <Mark at="cells:3,3-3,4">LEN − / LEN +: change pattern length</Mark>
  <Caption>In the SEQ FN menu, row 3 switches step pages and adjusts pattern length.</Caption>
</Shot>

- **Change length**: adjust **STEPS** on the TRACK page (<Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> for single steps, <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> to jump to page boundaries), or use **LEN − / LEN +** in the FN menu (steps through 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 48... up to 256).
- **Turn step pages**: press <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> on the note page with no steps selected (the right strip icons switch to → ← and a `PAGE n/m` banner appears), or use **PAGE − / PAGE +** in the FN menu.
- **Switch patterns**: press <Key k="FN+ADD" /> / <Key k="FN+SUB" /> on the note page with no steps selected to move to the next or previous pattern (queued until the loop finishes if playing, showing `NEXT PATTERN`).
- During playback, the GRID follows the playhead across pages automatically. Once you manually flip to a page, it stays locked there until the next time you press PLAY (and pages never auto-flip while a step is selected).
- New patterns inherit the current pattern's length, and duplicated patterns keep their length. Steps beyond the pattern's length stay unlit on the GRID.
- Note memory is allocated only for pages that actually contain notes. If memory runs out, Cardenza displays `MEMORY FULL - NOTE NOT WRITTEN`.

## Copy, paste, clear, quantize {#seq-tools}

Hold <Key k="FN" /> with a letter shortcut, or choose **COPY**, **PASTE**, **CLEAR**, **QUANT**, or **DUP** from the FN menu (each tile's second line shows whether it targets `STEPS` or `PATTERN`). On the note page, these operate on selected steps if any are selected; otherwise (and on PATTERN/TRACK pages), they apply to the entire pattern.

  <Group keys="T">Tap tempo</Group>

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| <Key k="FN+C" /> / <Key k="FN+V" /> | Copy / paste (PASTE stays inactive until you copy something) |
| <Key k="FN+X" /> | Clear (prompts for confirmation when clearing a whole pattern) |
| <Key k="FN+Q" /> | Quantize notes to whole steps |
| <Key k="FN+D" /> | Duplicate current pattern to the next available slot |
| <Key k="FN+T" /> | Tap tempo |

The SEQ FN menu is identical across all three SEQ pages:

| Row | Menu items |
|---|---|
| 1 | COPY · PASTE · CLEAR · QUANT |
| 2 | DUP · VIEW (ROLL / STEP) · RECORD (RECORD panel) · SCOPE (<Key k="FN+OK" />, when steps are selected) |
| 3 | PAGE − · PAGE + (note page, patterns > 16 steps) · LEN − · LEN + |
| 4 | SAVE · TAP · REC · SETUP (standard global row) |

See [Menus by screen](./fn.mdx#fn-menus) for details.

:::warning

:::
