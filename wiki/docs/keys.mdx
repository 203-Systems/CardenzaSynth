# Keys

Cardenza has a 4×14 matrix of backlit keys split into four color-coded blocks, plus a side **HOME** button. Every key has a single-color LED that shows what it can do in the current context.

## Left keys: ESC, TAB, FN, CTRL {#keys-left}

All four left-column keys follow the same pattern: **tap** to step or toggle, **hold** to open a visual map on the GRID.

| Key | <Icon name="tap" /> Tap | <Icon name="hold" /> Hold |
|---|---|---|
| <Key k="ESC" /> | Cycle scenes (<Scene name="INSTR" /> → <Scene name="SEQ" /> → <Scene name="TAPE" />); exit <Scene name="SETUP" />; cancel a dialog | Open the scene map on the GRID (column 1 opens 0 SETUP) |
| <Key k="TAB" /> | Next track (<Track n="1" /> → <Track n="2" /> → <Track n="3" /> → <Track n="4" />) | Open the track map (select, mute, solo, sequencer on/off) |
| <Key k="FN" /> | Toggle the current screen's context menu open or closed | Show the FN menu and enable secondary functions (see [The FN menu](./fn.mdx#fn-menu)); in input screens, use yellow key legends (see [FN in input screens: yellow legends](./fn.mdx#fn-yellow)) |
| <Key k="CTRL" /> | Next page | Open the page map for the current scene |

  <Mark at="cells:1,4-4,4">3 TAPE</Mark>

The four strips along the left edge of the screen correspond to these keys from top to bottom (ESC red-orange, TAB cyan, FN yellow, CTRL violet). Your current scene and page are shown in the status bar and page tabs, while the strips show key availability:

| Strip | Icon | State |
|---|---|---|
| <Key k="ESC" /> | Back arrow | Half lit (always available for scenes, back, or cancel) |
| <Key k="TAB" /> | Tab arrow | Off in pop-ups; briefly lights the matching row after switching tracks (T1 = ESC row through T4 = CTRL row) |
| <Key k="FN" /> | Menu | Fully lit while the FN menu is toggled open |
| <Key k="CTRL" /> | Pages | Off on single-page screens or when a pop-up is open |

Holding any of these keys fills its strip from the bottom up.

## Right keys: ADD, SUB, OK, PLAY {#keys-right}

<Keymap>
  <Group keys="ADD SUB">Increase / decrease (hold to repeat)</Group>
  <Group keys="OK">Confirm and SEQ record key</Group>
  <Group keys="PLAY">Play / stop</Group>
  <Caption>Far-right column: value controls on top, confirm and transport on the bottom.</Caption>
</Keymap>

| Key | Keycap | Function |
|---|---|---|
| <Key k="ADD" /> | del | Increase a value or move up in a list; hold to repeat with acceleration |
| <Key k="SUB" /> | `\` | Decrease a value or move down in a list |
| <Key k="OK" /> | ↵ Enter | Confirm, open, or toggle; on the SEQ note page it acts as the <Icon name="rec" /> **record key** (tap to toggle ROLL/STEP, hold 0.45 s for the RECORD panel) |
| <Key k="PLAY" /> | space | Start or stop playback; press <Key k="FN+PLAY" /> to arm or disarm live recording |

- Hold <Key k="TAB" /> and press <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to step to the next or previous track. Hold <Key k="CTRL" /> and press <Key k="ADD" /> / <Key k="SUB" /> to step to the next or previous page.
- The icons on the right screen strips show what ADD and SUB do on the current screen: ▲ ▼ for adjusting values or scrolling lists (ADD raises values and moves up), and → ← for horizontal navigation (such as SEQ step pages, engine selection, or stepping with TAB/CTRL held).

:::note

During text entry, the top-right key (marked `del`) acts as Backspace.

:::

## The PADS {#keys-pads}

The 4×8 block of keys in the middle always plays the **active track**, regardless of which scene or page you are on. There are four exceptions:
- While holding <Key k="ESC" />, <Key k="TAB" />, or <Key k="CTRL" /> (viewing a map), the pads are muted.
- While holding <Key k="FN" />, the pads trigger letter shortcuts and quick controls (see [Secondary functions and shortcuts](./fn.mdx#fn-shortcuts)).
- While holding a parameter cell on the GRID, the pads act as direct value selectors for that parameter.
- Inside preset and sample lists, the pads are muted so stray taps do not trigger anything (press <Key k="FN+F" /> to search).

## The GRID {#keys-grid}

The 4×4 GRID on the right maps 1:1 to the 4×4 cell layout in the middle of the screen. Whatever is drawn in a cell on screen is controlled by the key in that same position.

<Strip>
  <Step keys="G2" shot="seq-step" at="116,14,113,108">Sequencer: the 16 keys represent 16 steps (in STEP view, the right half of the screen shows the matching 4×4 layout). Patterns up to 256 steps are displayed one 16-step page at a time.</Step>
  <Step keys="G1" shot="instr-juno-vcf" at="cell:1,1">Instrument pages: each cell is a parameter; tap its key to focus it.</Step>
  <Step keys="G6" shot="tape-mix" at="cells:1,2-4,2">MIX: each column controls one track's select, mute, solo, and sequencer switches.</Step>
</Strip>

When you hold any left-column modifier key, the GRID temporarily becomes that key's navigation map or menu (see [Hold a left key for a map](./navigate.mdx#nav-maps)).

## HOME side button {#keys-home}

Pressing the **HOME** button on the left side of the device opens the system menu (Cardenza Synth 0.2.1 and newer), where you can choose **Back to Launcher**, **Restart**, **Power off**, or **Download mode**. It is not used for playback control inside the synth.

To stop everything immediately (<Icon name="stop" /> **All Stop**), press <Key k="FN+ESC" />. This stops the sequencer, kills any stuck notes, halts all arpeggiators, and displays `ALL STOP`.

:::note

Firmware installation, updates, and switching apps are handled by the 203 Launcher. Press <Key k="HOME" /> and choose **Back to Launcher** (on firmware older than 0.2.1, hold <Key k="FN" /> while plugging in USB until the Launcher screen appears). Do not hold **HOME** while plugging in USB unless you intend to perform a recovery flash; doing so boots the chip into download mode with a blank screen (unplug and reconnect to exit).

:::

## Key behavior rules {#keys-rules}

| Keys | Behavior |
|---|---|
| <Key k="hold ESC" /> <Key k="hold TAB" /> <Key k="hold CTRL" /> | Holding a left key turns the GRID into a selection map. Press the GRID key matching the item you want. |
| <Key k="hold FN" /> | While FN is held, normal key functions are suspended. The GRID shows the context menu, keys with FN actions light up and run only those actions, and all other keys are off and inactive. |
| <Icon name="tap" /> | Using a modifier key in a combo suppresses its tap action when you release it. |
| <Icon name="hold" /> | A hold registers after 250 ms (0.45 s for OK on the SEQ note page). Holding <Key k="ADD" /> or <Key k="SUB" /> starts auto-repeating after 0.4 s and switches to steps of 5 after 12 repeats. |
